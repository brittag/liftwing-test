Details about default scoring method
Factor Points Source of info Rationale
Reference need +0–40 LiftWing multilingual reference-need API Higher % of uncited sentences that need citations = lower credibility
Page views +0–40 Average daily page views over the last 7 days (log-scaled) Problems on high-traffic articles affect more readers
Contentious topic +40 Talk page notices or categories that signal a contentious topic Priority because these tend toward POV-pushing
Violence +20 Categories related to murder, abuse, slavery, human trafficking, terrorism, sexual assault, political violence, or war crimes More likely to have POV issues; need to check for WP:CRIME, WP:BLP1E, WP:AVOIDVICTIM
Tagged as AI-generated +20 Category:Articles containing suspected AI-generated texts WP:NOLLM
ChatGPT in references +20 Presence of utm_source=chatgpt.com Likely to have issues related to LLM generation
BLP likely a minor +20 Year-of-birth categories that indicate age 18 or younger Risk of harm if these articles have issues (stacks with BLP)
Biography of a living person +10 Category:Living people Issues can harm people; articles may involve COI/UPE
Tagged for notability issues +10 Category:All articles with topics of unclear notability May need to be draftified or nominated for deletion
Tagged as promotional +10 Category:All articles with a promotional tone May need some sort of action
Tagged as COI +10 Category:Wikipedia articles with possible conflicts of interest May need to be draftified or nominated for deletion
Tagged as having POV issues +10 Category:All Wikipedia neutral point of view disputes May need some sort of action
Orphaned article +10 Zero incoming links Less likely to have been seen by other editors
Creator is blocked +10 Creator account is blocked Not a good sign
Page length +0–10 Source text byte length (log-scaled) Long new articles are more often LLM-generated or POV-pushing than short ones
Article older than 90 days +5 Page creation date > 90 days ago Older unreviewed pages are indexed by search engines
Tagged as orphan +5 Orphan category present and at least one incoming link Suggests attention is needed
Sports −5 Categories related to sports Most athlete biographies and sports-event articles are not very sensitive or controversial
AfC −10 Accepted AfC submission Someone already reviewed it via Articles for Creation
Creator is NPP or AfC reviewer −10 Creator is on the NPP or AfC reviewer lists Hopefully they know what they're doing
Disambiguation page −20 Category:Disambiguation pages Disambiguation pages are unlikely to be controversial
