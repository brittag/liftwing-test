Tone checker

Tone Check detects the presence of promotional, derogatory, or otherwise subjective language. It's the core of the Revise Tone task presented to newcomers and users of Suggestion Mode.

Can we also use it to predict which articles in a list have tone problems? It kind of works. You can try it! But checking is slow, so this tool is separate from the other sorting tools.

Paste Wikipedia article titles (one per line) to count paragraphs with Tone Check probability ≥ 0.80 (within the first 20 paragraphs in the article) and compare to presence of related cleanup templates (promo, peacock, POV). You can check up to 100 articles at a time.
